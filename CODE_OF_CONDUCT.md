# Code of Conduct

Native UI is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect different backgrounds, abilities, identities, and experience levels.
- Keep discussions relevant and protect private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this code is violated. Reports should be sent privately through GitHub's security/contact facilities rather than posted publicly. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as practical and respond proportionately to verified incidents.
